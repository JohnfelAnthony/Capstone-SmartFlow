# Site A West → North loop and road closures: acceptance, 4 November 2026

**Evidence kind: synthetic demand only.** Seeded demand with the provisional Tagum vehicle mix; no field counts. Site A's loop is provisional OpenStreetMap geometry, not field-checked, and its side streets start empty (no traffic observed or generated there). Nothing here is field validation or a claim about real traffic.

- Engine `native-9`, source commit `fb6e3f9` (code unchanged since; later commits add only this report and documentation).
- Machine-readable results: [`site-a-closure-acceptance-2026-11-04.json`](site-a-closure-acceptance-2026-11-04.json) (16 runs), [`closures-2026-11-04/site-a-closure-effects-600s-seed7.json`](closures-2026-11-04/site-a-closure-effects-600s-seed7.json) (12 short runs).
- Cases: Site A at **medium and high** traffic, **no signal** and **fixed-time**, **without and with a closure**, 1,800 s, seeds 101 and 102, `static` routing (only closures move traffic onto the loop). The closure is the study junction's west inbound piece (`road2r`, Capitol Circumferential Road) closed from 300 s to 1,200 s, so West traffic must use the side-street loop. Generated by `tools/mixed_traffic_report.py --only siteA-static-<density>-<control> --determinism`, overlap checked at every tick.

## Targets: zero overlaps, stuck vehicles, gridlock and conservation error

**Met in all 16 runs** (8 cases × 2 seeds): `max_overlapping_pairs` 0, `max_stuck_junction_vehicles` 0, `max_gridlock_seconds` 0, `final_vehicle_conservation_error` 0. Determinism: two builds of each of the 8 cases gave equal state digests over 120 s, and both builds of the four medium closure cases × 2 seeds gave equal digests over the full 1,800 s.

Reading the table honestly:

- **The loop works as a detour.** With the piece closed, West traffic used the loop (11–69 trips per run) and completion stayed close to the no-closure case (medium no-signal: 493 vs 496 and 444 vs 444). No vehicle was diverted to a different exit and none was blocked, because every origin still had a route.
- **Reroutes are few because most traffic is routed at spawn.** Closed mid-run at 300 s, only vehicles already on the 15 m west entry stub needed a reroute (0 at medium, 1–2 at high). Closed from the start, a vehicle is routed round the loop when it spawns, so the reroute count is 0 by construction; the tests on the real network cover both (`tests/test_site_a_loop.py`).
- **High traffic is above capacity.** Demand of 2,340 vehicles an hour is far more than a one-lane-per-direction Site A completes (about 400 fixed-time, about 600 no-signal in 1,800 s), so hundreds of requests wait at the boundary (`pending_vehicles` 219–488) whether or not a road is closed. This matches the earlier native-8 report (607 / 593 no-signal) and says nothing new about the loop. Medium fixed-time also leaves 114–151 unfinished at the end.
- **`max_closure_held_vehicles` was 1 in three high-traffic closure runs.** It is one tick (0.1 s) at 300.0 s, when a car queued on the 15 m entry stub had the closure applied before its first reroute check; it was rerouted on the next tick (checked by stepping seed 102 through the event). It is not a stuck vehicle.
- **`longest_completion_gap_s` is 59–75 s in every run, equal with and without closure for each seed,** so it is the start-up time before the first trip completes, not a freeze.
- **Widening did not visibly change the study junction.** Medium no-signal completions were 496 / 444 here against 492 / 457 on the one-junction network (native-8 report), and medium fixed-time 374 / 351 against 362 / 329 (native-7 report). These are different engine builds, so this is a sanity check, not a comparison.

Before this work (audit, native-8, one-junction Site A) a whole closed road completed 81 of 167 trips with 86 never entering and no report. Now the loop carries West traffic round the closed piece, vehicles whose exit is closed divert and are counted, and demand whose entry road is closed is reported (`closure_blocked_vehicles`) instead of waiting silently.

## Limits

- Synthetic demand, provisional geometry, no field calibration; the 346 m survey figure includes 47 m of Capitol Circumferential Road, and the loop street itself is 275 m with its corner rounded (25 m) because a square corner overlapped.
- One closure position (the west inbound piece) was run at 1,800 s. Other pieces were run for 600 s only (table above), and the other three directions have no loop.
- The west boundary stub beyond the loop junction is 24 m (a 15 m lane); this did not limit these runs, but a long queue there would stop West demand entering.
- A vehicle with no open way out is held and reported, not removed; none occurred in these runs apart from the one-tick case above.
