## Decisions I made

1. **A third demand source, `field_counts`,** instead of `observed` (held-out duplicate check) or `synthetic` (would hide that the traffic comes from counts).
2. **Approach naming is the site's own** (south, north, east, west) on the counting lines' `approach_id`; an exit line's `approach_id` names the leg it counts vehicles leaving on, and exits are lines with `role` "exit". This avoids a separate mapping file, and puts a requirement on how the lines are named in Field Observer (see unchecked).
3. **Poisson streams, not replay.** Each bin is a seeded Poisson stream at the bin's rate, as the existing demand windows are; exact event times are not replayed. The older trips converter does replay but needs a mapping, upstream speeds and an observed empty start.
4. **Vehicles seen in the last 60 s of footage with no exit yet are left out and reported;** earlier vehicles with no exit are refused (their turn is unknown).
5. **The two ledger templates were deleted with the criteria** (they were the gate's evidence file). Easy to restore from git if you want them kept.
6. **Manifest hashes are checked on the five uploaded files only;** a real manifest also lists files that are not uploaded, which are not checked.

## Not fixed, not checked

- **No real Field Observer export was read.** I do not know whether the GUI's `lines.json` carries `role: "exit"` and the site's approach names in `approach_id` by default; if not, the user must set them when drawing the lines, and the refusal says what is wrong. Worth one check against a real revision.
- **Demand is a steady hourly rate** (owner's decision, applied after this report): each movement and class runs at count × 3600 / span for the whole run, so runs longer than the 16-minute footage keep receiving vehicles; within-period variation is not modelled. (This report first replayed per-bin rates and stopped when the counts ended.)
- **No frontend unit test for the new component;** it was checked in the browser (preview, apply, save, a refusal) at Site A only, with an invented export.
- **Import → scenario → train → evaluate → compare → report** end to end is stage 5's rehearsal; here only the engine run on the imported demand and the smoke-test training path were exercised.
- Archival documents (history, review notes, earlier plans, earlier stage reports) still describe the removed gate and page as they were.
