## Architecture boundaries

- React/TypeScript/Vite and Canvas 2D provide interaction and rendering. Python/FastAPI owns simulation and learning.
- The active engine is `simulation/traffic_engine.py`; do not substitute similarly named legacy engines or reconnect SUMO/TraCI.
- `engine_config` is the native scenario contract. Preserve complete saved settings through editing, running, training and evaluation.
- RL currently controls one selected junction; routing is separate. The two study sites (`tagum_site_a`, `tagum_site_b`) are provisional OpenStreetMap networks and neither has a signal; the no-signal (`first_come`) model is a hypothesis. There is no calibration and no validation (D38): results are "scenario-based, demand from field counts, not calibrated or validated", and the counts come from one 16-minute midday video per site (Scenarios, Import counts from Field Observer, `services/field_observer_counts.py`; demand source `field_counts`). Study-site training and evaluation run on any saved scenario; `--synthetic-smoke-test` is a code check that marks the artifact. Label synthetic evidence as synthetic, and field-count evidence with its limits.
- Supported simulation classes are car, motorcycle, tricycle, bus and truck, plus separate pedestrian support. The retired emergency feature must not be restored through legacy settings; models require the current contract: engine `native-10`, observation `native-local-20-v4`, actions `phase-service-5-v2` and the model's recorded `phase_plan`. `native-8` and earlier models and recordings (including `native-6` / `native-local-22-v3`) are history: refuse them, do not relabel them. Signalized and all-way-stop traffic is unchanged from `native-6` (pinned in `tests/test_native6_compat.py`), and traffic without closures is unchanged from `native-8` in the pinned runs (`tests/test_closure_free_digests.py`; `native-10`, D34, fixes engine faults found by review and adds the `stalled_vehicles` alarm). Road closures (D33): users choose closed roads in the Scenarios page; a closure that leaves no legal movement is refused; vehicles whose destination is cut off divert to the best open exit and are counted (`diverted_vehicles`); a run at a study site is `static` (reacts to closures, not congestion) unless the scenario has a closure whose response (`engine_config.closure_responses`, chosen on the clickable junction in the Scenarios form) is "avoid jams", which makes the whole run adaptive; `validate_config` sets this for every path, a saved `adaptive` scenario with no such closure runs static (D35). Site A models every public street in the owner's crop around the public market (D41, `build_site_a_street_grid`): 13 junctions, all first-come except the study junction `tagum_site_a_j`, which stays first and the only signal and RL candidate. Generated traffic starts and ends only at the four main-road ends and generated pedestrians cross only at Site A, j2 and j3 (`generated_demand: false` elsewhere); side streets (`minor`) cost `MINOR_ROUTE_FACTOR` times their driving time in route choice, so they carry traffic only when a closure forces a detour. Shared-lane closure (D36, hypothetical): a closed direction may use half of the opposite pavement (response `share`, derived at run time in `simulation/contraflow.py`, the base network and its fingerprint unchanged, lanes drained before each change); eligible roads are two-way, at least 6.0 m per direction and 60 m long (Site A's Capitol Circumferential and Dalisay Gante, Site B's Sobrecarey; the National Highway is not built); always label it hypothetical. Known limit (D37): a rare side-by-side freeze at a stop line (1 of 8 seeds at Site A in stressed closure cases) is accepted; the strict xfail in `tests/test_site_a_loop.py` is its record, and any run with `stalled_vehicles` above zero is excluded or reported, never silently used.
- Live runs and recordings made from Scenarios or the Dashboard start with a hidden warm-up (D39, `engine_config.warmup_seconds`, default 120 s, none for scripted traffic): the engine's clock, events, demand and seed stay absolute as in an evaluation with that warm-up, while frames, the event log and charts carry `visible_time` (0 when the warm-up ends) and metrics count from there. Training, `tools/evaluate_controllers.py` and playback keep their own warm-up arguments. Recordings made this way have no warm-up frames and pair in Compare Runs only with recordings made the same way.
- This application currently uses process-local live/training runtimes. Do not assume multiple API workers share that state.
